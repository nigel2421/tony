How to maintain spring machine？


Correct maintenance is an important link to ensure the normal operation of the spring machinery, reduce the failure rate, and extend its service life.


In order to reduce the wear rate of spring mechanical parts, eliminate long-term hidden troubles, and extend the service life of machinery, the following work needs to be done:


一. Routine maintenance. Routine maintenance is centered on cleaning, tightening, adjusting, and lubricating. Before, during and after work, routine spring machine maintenance should be performed as required. This task is performed independently by the spring machine operator.


二. Regular maintenance. According to the maintenance requirements of various mechanical equipment, after reaching the specified working time or the specified mileage, the corresponding regular maintenance must be carried out, including the following:


First-level maintenance: First-level maintenance is carried out on the basis of routine maintenance. The focus is on lubrication, tightening and checking that the relevant parts have been cleaned and the three filters have been cleaned (air, engine oil, fuel filter). Mainly guided by the head of the mechanical team, completed by mechanical operators.


Secondary maintenance: Secondary maintenance mainly focuses on inspection and adjustment. Specifically check the working conditions of the engine, clutch, transmission, etc. and make necessary adjustments to eliminate the faults found. Ensure that spring machinery has good working performance. The second-level maintenance is done by the mechanical operator to assist the warranty personnel.


Three-level maintenance: the focus is on detection, adjustment, and elimination of hidden troubles. Diagnose and inspect the parts that affect the performance, and then complete the necessary replacement, adjustment and troubleshooting.


Luoyang Xianheng spring machinery warm tips，in addition to these maintenance methods，Also be careful not to do a lot of springs with overloaded wire diameters, that will go against the maintenance of the spring machine. Don't use the new machine as a training experiment for novice.
