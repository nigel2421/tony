How to deal with oil stains on the surface of stainless steel springs？


When the stainless steel spring is used for a period of time, or when the spring is just produced from the machine, oil stains will inevitably appear on the surface. If proper methods are not used when dealing with the oil stains, not only will the oil stains not be removed, but the springs will be damaged. So how do spring manufacturers remove oil from stainless steel springs? The following three methods for removing oil stains on the surface of stainless steel springs will be introduced by the manufacturer of Xianheng Spring Machinery:

1. Put the stainless steel spring into the cleaning agent diluted with clean water. The dilution ratio of clean water to cleaning agent is 1:1 or 1:2. The soaking time should not be too long. After the surface of the stainless steel spring is free of oil, take it out and rinse with clean water. , So that the stainless steel spring can be restored to the matt effect.

2. The ratio of cleaning agent to clean water in ultrasonic equipment is about 1:30. The soaking time should not be too long. After the surface of the stainless steel spring is free of oil, take it out and rinse it with clean water, so that the stainless steel spring can restore the matte effect.

3. Put the stainless steel spring into a vibrating finishing machine with coarse abrasive and cleaning agent. After grinding and polishing the spring, take out the stainless steel spring and clean it with water. The surface of the spring will not only have no scratches, but will also have a smoother finish.


Therefore, the spring must be properly handled during production. During use, the corrosion damage of the spring must be avoided to maintain its performance.
