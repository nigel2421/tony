How to eliminate the problem of spring machine probe?


Computer spring machine in the process of production line lost routine state，causes problems with irregular products. In the daily production, we have observed that problems like this are caused by the malfunction of the probe in the spring machine, so how should we eliminate the problem of the probe? Henan Luoyang Xianheng spring machine to give the following guidance：


1、Electrical signal failure: the probe will stop the auxiliary wire feed without touching the steel wire (maybe the electrical signal wire is not insulated from the machine), or the probe will not stop sending the wire when it touches the steel wire (maybe the electrical signal wire is in a disconnected state). It is necessary to check whether the electrical signal of the control needle is normal, mainly whether the electrical signal wire of the probe is insulated from the surrounding, or whether the electrical signal wire is open. (At this time, you can remove the probe and hold it in your hand. When the machine is running until the probe is working, then take the probe to touch the device to see if the wire feeding can be stopped immediately. If not, then prove that the control needle work abnormal.


2、Observe the working status of the screen analysis probe: Observe the working status of the 1/0 interface signal light at the bottom right corner of the screen when the probe is installed. If the probe senses the steel wire, it should be: the probe stretches out. 1 The blue signal light on the left is on; then the probe senses the steel wire, the light on the left is off, and the yellow signal light on the right is on. (It may be necessary to add the pause time T command before the cylinder retracts (S1-) to see the yellow signal light.
