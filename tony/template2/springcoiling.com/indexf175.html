The characteristics and classification of conical spring


Conical spring is a kind of shock-absorbing spring. Its main features are small volume, large load and variable stiffness. It is widely used in occasions with small space and large load and shock-absorbing devices. The following is the classification and characteristics of conical springs for everyone:


Classification of conical springs:


Conical springs are divided into three forms: equal helix angle, equal pitch, and equal stress.
